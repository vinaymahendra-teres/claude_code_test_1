## Stream 7 — Automation Opportunities

> **Status (2026-05-24):** **Unified cottage stack complete.** All 5 Google Sheets module files built, 9 Apps Scripts shipped: CRM Phase 0 (`form_handler` + `capacity_helpers` + `birthday_reminder` + `feedback_request` + `festival_pre_block`), Finance Phase 1a-1e (`upi_reconciler` + `compliance_reminder`), Operations Phase 2 (`bake_plan_generator` + `par_breach_alerter`), Marketing Phase 3 (no Apps Scripts), Dashboard Phase 4 (`morning_digest`). Tooling opex: ₹0/month. See [`specs/2026-05-24-cottage-stack-design.md`](specs/2026-05-24-cottage-stack-design.md) for the architecture and the 5 `<module>_strategy.md` docs for per-module detail.

### Current pain points (where time leaks)

- **Order management:** addressed — `Inquiries` → `Customers` → `Orders` pipeline in Sheets; Google Form intake; click-to-chat outreach.
- **Customer history / repeat tracking:** addressed — `Customers` tab with derived `lifetime_orders`, `lifetime_value`, `last_order_date`.
- **Recipe scaling and per-order costing:** **out of scope for Stream 7.** Belongs in Stream 1 (tooling) / Stream 3 (vendor & inventory). CRM only holds `final_price` and `customisation_notes`.
- **Production scheduling (daily bake plan):** partial — `Capacity Calendar` rolls up Orders by delivery_date; full daily bake plan derives from this, deferred to Stream 1.
- **Payment reconciliation across UPI / cash / bank:** manual in `Orders` (deposit_received, balance_received, balance_due formula). Full reconciliation deferred until Stream 2 commits to a books platform.
- **Marketing post scheduling:** out of Stream 7 scope at this stage; belongs in Stream 5.

### Existing infrastructure to leverage

- **Tools currently in use:** Google Workspace (Sheets, Forms, Apps Script, Gmail). WhatsApp Business. Instagram. Odoo (NTPL — not used for bakery).
- **Comfort level with light internal tooling vs SaaS:** comfortable with Sheets + Apps Script; explicitly avoiding SaaS CRMs (Zoho/HubSpot/etc.) at this stage to keep monthly opex at zero.

### Build vs buy preference

- **Pain point most worth automating first:** the T+2 Google review request (`feedback_request.gs`). Single highest-ROI automation for Hyderabad home-bakery SEO. Installed in Week 8 of the rollout.
- **Build vs buy direction:** Build (Sheets + Apps Script) is the steady state. Re-evaluate platform only if volume signals in [`crm_strategy.md`](crm_strategy.md) §1 fire — and most are absorbed within Sheets via the patterns in [`crm_strategy.md`](crm_strategy.md) §11.
